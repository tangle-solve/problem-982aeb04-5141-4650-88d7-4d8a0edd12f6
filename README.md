### ALGO-000158: Near-quadratic complexity of 3D line Voronoi diagrams
- **Subfield:** Computational geometry
- **Open:** Prove an \(O(n^{2+\varepsilon})\) complexity bound, for every fixed \(\varepsilon>0\), for Euclidean Voronoi diagrams of \(n\) lines in three dimensions, or find a superquadratic counterexample.
- **Status:** Quadratic lower bounds and substantially larger upper bounds leave a longstanding gap.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Determines the feasible output complexity of spatial proximity among linear features.
- **Key works:** Sharir (1994), *Almost Tight Upper Bounds for Lower Envelopes in Higher Dimensions*; TOPP Problem 3.
- **Related:** Computational geometry of robotics—proximity to edges and axes.
